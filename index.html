What Is the Exness Personal Area?

The Exness Personal Area is the account-management section available to eligible Exness clients. Depending on the user's region, account status, and applicable Exness entity, it may provide access to trading accounts, profile and verification information, payment options, and platform-related settings.

Available services and account conditions can differ between countries and legal entities. The official Exness website and the terms shown in a client's own Personal Area should be treated as the current reference.

Exness MT4 and MT5 Login: What's the Difference?

Exness MT4 Login

To connect an eligible MT4 trading account, enter the trading account number, trading password, and the correct Exness server in MetaTrader 4. These are not the same as the email and password used for the Personal Area.

Exness MT5 Login

MT5 also uses trading-account credentials and a matching server. Exness explains that MT5 credentials are platform-specific and cannot be used to log in to MT4. Check the platform and account type before signing in.

Exness describes MetaTrader 5 as a trading terminal. For account management and payment actions, it directs clients to its own account-management tools, including the Exness Trade app where available. Platform features and availability depend on region and account eligibility.

Exness platforms and market access

Exness lists several ways to access trading services, including web, mobile, and desktop experiences. Its website currently highlights MetaTrader 5, Exness Terminal, and the Exness Trade app. Instruments and features vary by jurisdiction and account.

How to Keep Your Exness Account Secure

Protecting account access is essential when using any online financial service. Use a unique password, keep your device and browser updated, and avoid signing in through links in unexpected emails or messages. Check the domain carefully before entering account information. Do not share your password, one-time passcodes, or recovery details with anyone.

Exness's client agreement places responsibility on clients to protect their access data and to report suspected unauthorized disclosure promptly. If you believe someone has accessed your account, use the official Exness support route and follow its security instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find the official Exness login?

Use the sign-in page linked from the official Exness website. The direct sign-in address currently indexed by Exness is my.exness.com/accounts/sign-in.

Are Personal Area and MT4/MT5 passwords the same?

No. The Personal Area uses the registered email and Personal Area password. MT4 and MT5 use trading-account credentials and the relevant server details.

Can I use MT5 credentials in MT4?

Exness states that MT5 credentials are exclusive to that platform and cannot be used to access MT4.

Does Exness offer services in the United States?

Exness's official regional restrictions state that its group does not provide services to residents of the USA. Check the latest official terms for current information.

What should I do if I cannot sign in?

Check your credentials and platform/server selection, use the official password-reset process if needed, and contact Exness through its official website for account-specific assistance.
